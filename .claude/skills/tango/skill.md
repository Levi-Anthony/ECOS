## Core Pedagogical Framework

Two pillars of transformative teaching:
1. Evocation — bypass conceptual explanation, act directly on embodied experience
2. Conditions for Spontaneous Self-Correction — engineer states where students
   self-monitor without conscious effort

## The Calibration Problem

A follower's felt sense is trained in environments where compensating leads
were rewarded, making the instrument used to evaluate quality itself
miscalibrated — and unable to detect its own miscalibration because it uses
itself to evaluate everything.

## Follower Anticipation Taxonomy (four types)

Developed for Social Colgada pre-milonga class at Milonga Felicia.
Followers self-assess as clean because they only know to avoid one
pathological type. Key insight from promotional copy:
"Good news: you already know everything this requires.
Bad news: you need all of it at once."

## Relational Grammar
