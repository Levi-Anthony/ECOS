# Technique: The Micro-Reset Protocol
**Tags:** #Technique #SocialFloor #EmergencyProtocol #Navigation
**Related:** [[Concept - The Musician's Water]]

## 🎯 The Delivery (Student Facing)
*The "Emergency Brake" for when a dance starts spiraling.*

> "If you trip, disconnect, or just feel 'off,' don't try to dance through it. That just builds more tension. **Stop. Reset. Restart.**"

**The 3-Second Reset Routine (Use *during* the song):**
1.  **The Pause:** Stop all linear movement immediately. Keep the embrace, but anchor your feet. Don't wiggle.
2.  **The Exhale:** Take a visible breath *out*. Drop your heels. Drop your shoulders. (This signals to your partner: "We are safe, I am here, I am calm.")
3.  **The Re-Connect:** Shift your weight clearly to *one* foot (Find the [[Concept - The Available Foot (W1)|Available Foot]]). Wait for the next clear beat. Start again.

## 🧬 The Rigor (Teacher's Backend)
* **Physiological Regulation:** A forced exhale stimulates the **Vagus Nerve**, shifting the nervous system from Sympathetic (Fight/Flight/Freeze) to Parasympathetic (Rest/Digest).
* **Proprioception:** "Dropping the heels" lowers the Center of Mass (COM), physically grounding the dancer to override the mental scattering.

## 🔎 Clarification
This is not a failure; it is a **syncopation**. You are trading movement for presence.
