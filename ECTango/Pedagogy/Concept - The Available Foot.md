# Concept: The Available Foot & Unilateral Clarity
**Tags:** #Pedagogy/Beginner #Technique/Fundamentals #W1 #Drills
**Related:** [[Theory - The Rosetta Stone of Walking]]

## 🎯 The Delivery (Student Facing)
*The "Auto-magical" Cue. High leverage, low friction.*

> **"A step only works when one foot is in charge and the other is available."**

**The Script:**
* "Don't rush the readiness. Wait until you feel one foot in charge."
* "When that’s clear, your partner can feel your direction without guessing."

## 🧬 The Rigor (Teacher's Backend)
* **Mechanics:** This drill isolates the **Stance Phase** of the gait cycle.
* **Goal:** To cure "shuffling" (unintentional weight splitting) by enforcing a binary state: 100% committed or 0% free.
* **Proprioception:** Trains the inhibition of the *Stretch Reflex* in the free leg. The leg must remain neutral ("Available") until signaled.

## 🏛️ Comparative Context
* **The Traditional/Vague Cue:** "Step to the music." (Often leads to rushing/falling).
* **The Innovation:** Framing the step as a state of readiness ("In Charge") rather than an action ("Pushing").
* **Pedagogical Note:** This is a *diagnostic tool* for Week 1, not the final goal of Tango movement (see [[Theory - The Rosetta Stone of Walking]]).

## Application Context
- [x] **Teaching Drill:** "Stop and Check." Freeze the class; ask them to lift the free foot. If they have to shift weight to do it, they failed the "In Charge" check.
- [x] **Social Floor:** Navigation safety—you cannot pivot or stop safely if weight is split unintentionally.
