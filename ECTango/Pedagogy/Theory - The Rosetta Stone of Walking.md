## 🎯 The Delivery (Student Facing)
*The shift from "Robot" to "Human."*

> "The walk is the Tango. Walk like you are strolling in a plaza with a new love interest: relaxed, continuous, and letting the ground hold you up."

## 🧬 The Rigor (Teacher's Backend)
* **The Myth:** "Always maintain 100% Unilateral Stance / Never split weight."
* **The Reality:** Efficient human gait *requires* a **Double Support Phase** (~20% of cycle).
    * *Heel Strike (Leading)* + *Pre-Swing (Trailing)* = Stability.
* **Biomechanics:** Deleting double support creates a "choppy" inverted pendulum motion (Zombie Walk) and increases metabolic cost. Allowing the transition utilizes the skeletal structure for support, reducing tension in the hips/quads.

## 🏛️ Comparative Context
* **The Traditional Dogma:** Explicitly forbids split weight.
* **The "Aha!" Moment:** Realizing that "Unilateral Stance" is a drill to fix shuffling, but **Natural Gait** is the performance goal.
* **The Codex:** Nearly every advanced tango dynamic can be derived from the emotional and biomechanical reality of a normal, romantic stroll.

## Application Context
* **Pedagogical Arc:**
    1.  **Phase 1 (W1):** Enforce strict Unilateral Clarity (Tool: [[Concept - The Available Foot]]) to delete noise.
    2.  **Phase 2 (W3+):** Re-introduce Double Support (Natural Gait) to delete tension and robotic movement.
