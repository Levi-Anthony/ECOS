# Theory: Process Over Outcome (Curing Hesitation)
**Tags:** #Psychology #Mindset #Pedagogy #TeacherTraining
**Related:** [[Technique - The Micro Reset]]

## 🎯 The Delivery (Student Facing)
*The mental switch to cure "Lead's Paralysis."*

> **"Your partner doesn't need you to be perfect; they need you to be present."**

**The Trap (Outcome Goals):**
Thinking: *"Is this boring? Am I making mistakes? Do I look good?"*
* *Result:* Tension, hesitation, and "Analysis Paralysis." You are judging yourself, so you assume your partner is too.

**The Fix (Process Goals):**
Give your brain a small, concrete job that you can 100% control:
* *"Did I arrive fully on my standing foot?"*
* *"Did I breathe during that pause?"*
* *"Did I keep my chest up?"*

## 🧬 The Rigor (Teacher's Backend)
* **Cognitive Load Theory:** The brain has limited bandwidth. "Outcome" thoughts (fear of judgment) occupy working memory, leaving no room for motor planning.
* **Amygdala Hijack:** Fear of social embarrassment triggers a low-level "Freeze" response (co-contraction of muscles). This physical stiffness blocks the communication channel with the partner.
* **The Loop:** Negative Thought $\rightarrow$ Physical Tension $\rightarrow$ Blocked Signal $\rightarrow$ Partner Confusion $\rightarrow$ Confirmed Negative Thought.

## Application Context
* **Teaching Strategy:** When a student looks panicked, ask them: "What is your job right now?" If they say "To lead a turn," correct them. Their job is "To step clearly."
