# Protocol Hardening Audit Checklist

---

## Purpose
Use this checklist to document progress through the ten stages of the **Protocol Hardening Pipeline** when designing or reviewing a protocol.  
Each section contains short prompts and confirmation boxes for quick audits.

---

## 1. Scope Definition and Isolation
- [ ] One-sentence definition of what the protocol *is*.  
- [ ] One-sentence definition of what it *is not*.  
- [ ] Adjacent domains deferred to sibling protocols.
