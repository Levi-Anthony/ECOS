# 2025-11-11 · Practica Announcement & Ethos Discovery

## 📍 Context
End of 8-week class series; announcement made at práctica.  
Objective: invite attendees to final class, maintain warm community tone, and optionally reference the Tucson Tango Calendar without feeding past drama.

---

## 🗣️ Summary of Event
Levi delivered a spontaneous announcement that evolved from a logistical reminder into an **articulation of teaching ethos** — framing tango learning as an *ongoing process of shared discovery*.  
The tone was confident, open, and invitational. The audience response was positive and engaged.

---

## 💡 Key Points
- Teaching = ongoing conversation and discovery, not static authority.  
- Classes = structured explorations centered on one concept, anchored by a figure/exercise.  
- Practica = open space for dialogue, sharing, and continued exploration.  
- Framed invitation as “join the process,” not “come to class.”  
- Mentioned upcoming events: final class, Friday práctica, and Milonga V.  

---

## 🎙️ Reusable 30-Second Practica Announcement Script
> “Hey everyone — quick note. For me, tango’s not something you *master* once; it’s an ongoing conversation. Every time we dance or talk about it, we discover something new — together.  
>  
> That’s what I love about classes: they’re just structured explorations around one central idea, with exercises and a figure to help it come alive. If you’re curious about that process, come join us tomorrow night — it’s the last class of the session, and we’d love to have you in the mix.  
>  
> And of course, we’ve got our **Friday práctica** and **Milonga V** on Saturday — keep dancing, keep discovering.”  

**Variants:**  
- “Tomorrow night” → “this Wednesday,” “next round starts soon,” etc.  
- “Last class” → “new series starts,” “special topic,” etc.

---

## 🔍 Analysis
- **Tone success:** Warm, genuine, and subtly authoritative.  
- **Strategic success:** Reframed class invitation as philosophical participation.  
- **Brand alignment:** Embodies *Effortless Connection* core — exploration, clarity, and joy.  
- **Public perception:** Strengthens reputation as a teacher-philosopher grounded in real practice.
