## 1) Voice Pillars (the non-negotiable blend)

### Pillar A — Core Professionalism
Competent, measured, reliable. You respect the reader’s time.

**Signals:** clear structure, crisp logistics, specific outcomes, calm confidence, zero desperation.

---

### Pillar B — Expressive Edge
Human, vivid, slightly irreverent—without losing authority.

**Signals:** memorable phrasing, understated humor, warmth, a wink (never “dance-school cheer”).

**Benevolent Mischief (strategic option inside Pillar B):**  
A playful, truth-based wink that punctures unhelpful myths (mysticism, ego, overthinking) to make learning lighter—**without** reducing respect, safety, or professionalism.  
**Rule:** target **ideas**, not people.

**Measured / Calibrated Vulnerability (strategic option inside Pillar B):**  
1–2 sentences of bounded humanity that increases trust or relatability, then pivots back to service/outcome.  
**Rule:** never emotional processing, never oversharing, never inviting reassurance.

---

### Pillar C — Pedagogical Integrity
You teach seriously without being academic or intimidating.

**Signals:** “This is learnable,” fundamentals framed as advanced when done well, leaders/followers both addressed with respect, misconceptions corrected cleanly.

---

### Pillar D — Magnetic Presence (Rockstar Signal)
Understated star power: high competence + high taste + low noise.

**Signals:** restraint, selectivity, receipts (proof without bragging), iconic containers (naming), composure.

**Anti-cringe rule:** Rockstar is a *byproduct* of mastery and taste—not hype, urgency, or peacocking.

## 3) Message Blueprint (use this structure almost always)
1) **What it is** (one clean line)  
2) **Level Setting** (aspirational framing: the *stage of practice / state of mind*, not a deficiency)  
3) **What you’ll get** (specific tools/outcomes)  
4) **One signature line** (choose ONE: precision promise OR benevolent mischief OR measured vulnerability OR technical re-frame)  
5) **Logistics** (time/location/price)  
6) **Single clear CTA** (link / RSVP / reply)

**Rule:** If it gets longer, it must get *clearer*, not just “more.”

**Level Setting examples (aspirational, not remedial):**
- “For dancers ready to refine timing and texture.”  
- “For anyone who wants cleaner options without overthinking.”  
- “For leaders and followers who want navigation to feel effortless.”

- “Details + schedule: ⟦LINK⟧. RSVP: ⟦LINK⟧.”

### Pattern 7 — The Technical Re-Frame (Pedagogical Authority)
Use this to signal depth and solve problems immediately—without jargon.

**Concept:** name the common assumption, then state the actual cause (often counter-intuitive), then give the clean handle.
- “It’s not about holding your axis harder. It’s about releasing the tension that pushes you off it.”
- “Don’t lead the step. Lead the space for the step.”
- “It’s not ‘more energy.’ It’s cleaner timing.”

## 6) Do / Don’t (fast checklist)

**DO**
- Lead with experience + outcome.
- Use specific, believable micro-promises.
- Include exact logistics and a single CTA.
- Speak to leaders and followers explicitly.
- Use Mischief / Vulnerability / Technical Re-frame as *seasoning*, not a persona.
- Let restraint signal confidence.

**DON’T**
- Don’t sound transactional (“deal,” “steal,” “value,” “limited time”) unless truly necessary.
- Don’t over-explain theory in promo copy.
- Don’t lean on tango cliché soup (“passion,” “journey,” “magic”) without concrete meaning.
- Don’t shame skill gaps—normalize and solve.
- Don’t stack jokes or “try to be a rockstar.”

**Benevolent Mischief pass/fail**
1) Target is an **idea**, not a person.  
2) Reader feels **more capable**, not smaller.  
3) Line **increases clarity** or **reduces intimidation**.  
If any is “no,” cut it.

**Measured Vulnerability pass/fail**
1) 1–2 sentences.  
2) No details that invite follow-up.  
3) Immediate pivot back to outcome.  
If any is “no,” cut it.

## 9) Workflow (write fast without losing integrity)
**Pass 1 — Skeleton:** What / Level Setting / What they get / Logistics / CTA.  
**Pass 2 — Brand polish:** Add one micro-promise + one invitation line + (optional) ONE of: Mischief OR Vulnerability OR Technical Re-frame.

### Pass 3 — Subtraction Protocol (Rockstar Filter)
**Rule:** Confidence equals brevity.  
**Action:** Cut 10–20% of the word count from Pass 2.  
**Action:** Delete generic intensifiers (really, truly, amazing, super, very).  
**Test:** Does the sentence try to convince (marketing)? Or assume value (authority)?  
If it tries to convince, delete or compress.

**Final check:** calm, clean, specific, one CTA, no hype.
