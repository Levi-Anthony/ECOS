# Visual Explainer Development 1.3
- Price–Volume Interpretation (Project Brief)

---

## Objective

Develop a **textbook-style, two-page visual explainer** focused on *price–volume interpretation in trading charts*. The explainer should be suitable for inclusion in learning modules, print textbooks, or as a standalone digital handout. Prioritize clarity, accuracy, and visual intuitiveness at every stage.

Begin with a concise checklist (3–7 bullets) outlining the planned sub-tasks to structure the development process before substantive work begins.

Set `reasoning_effort = medium`, reflecting moderate conceptual and layout complexity. During workflow execution, keep outputs succinct and factual; provide expanded explanatory outputs upon finalization for downstream instructional use.

---

## Requirements

- Combine clean, minimalist diagrams with concise plain-language explanations.
- Organize content into the following themed sections:
  1. **Candlestick Anatomy**
  2. **Volume as Market Voice**
  3. **Price–Volume Matrix**
  4. **Divergence Patterns**
- Emphasize *practical utility* by delivering not just factual accuracy, but also actionable strategies, relevant context, and prompts for critical thinking.
- For each section, include:
  - Clear, accurate core concepts
  - At least one actionable insight or practical tip
  - Contextual notes (such as liquidity considerations, exceptions, or limitations)
  - Suggestions for further learning (optional, but encouraged)
- Maintain a visually balanced, modular layout suitable for both standalone handouts and presentation slides.

Default to plain text outputs unless markdown or styled diagrams are expressly requested, ensuring consistent cross-platform rendering. If markdown or styled diagrams are needed, use fenced code blocks for code and reserve markdown styling for section headers or emphasis as instructed.

## Project Workflow

1. **Define Objectives & Visual Style for Each Section**
   - Clarify learning goals and identify the most effective graphic approach for each topic.
2. **Draft Core Visual Components**
   - Sketch foundational diagrams and allocate dedicated spaces for action tips and explanatory notes.
3. **Write Plain-Language Explanations**
   - Introduce terms and concepts within context, minimizing trading-specific jargon.
4. **Design a Modular, Dual-Purpose Layout**
   - Ensure clarity for print and digital formats, dedicating space for narrative and supporting content blocks.
5. **Incorporate Mnemonics, Actionable Guidance & Contextual Notes**
   - Add memory aids, practical guidance, and notes about exceptions or market structure (such as liquidity); connect learning points to real-world trading practice and caution against common pitfalls.
6. **Fact-Check All Content**
   - Cross-reference all material with authoritative finance sources; clearly indicate any simplifications or possible areas of misunderstanding.
7. **Review for Instructional Usefulness & Coherence**
   - Assess whether the explainer empowers learners, addresses typical misconceptions, and revise for practical relevance and narrative consistency.
8. **Assess for Clarity & Visual Impact**
   - Review each section and the full project for clarity, logical flow, and visual communication.
9. **Prepare for Reusability & Expansion**
   - Ensure modularity in diagrams and text for future modifications or expansions.
