Zero → Creator

Day 1 Checklist (60 minutes total)

Objective: Define who you are for, what you’re about, and what success looks like — before you post anything.

⏱️ 0–10 min — Pick ONE primary platform (no hedging)

Action

- Choose one platform to focus on for the entire 4 weeks.

Rules

- Not “Instagram + TikTok.”
- Not “I’ll cross-post later.”
- One platform = faster feedback loops.

Heuristic

- If you want reach + discovery → TikTok / Reels
- If you want authority + depth → Twitter (X) / LinkedIn
- If you want community + vibes → Instagram

Output

- Write this sentence:  
    “For the next 30 days, I am a creator on [PLATFORM].”

⏱️ 10–20 min — Define your 

Audience of One

Action

- Describe ONE specific person you want your content to help.

Answer in bullets (short, concrete):

- Who are they? (role / identity)
- What problem annoys them weekly?
- What do they secretly want to be better at?
- What makes them skeptical of “content creators”?

Constraint

- If your audience could say “this is about me,” it’s good.
- If everyone could say that, it’s useless.

Output

- 3–5 bullet points. No polish.

⏱️ 20–30 min — Lock your creator POV (Point of View)

Action

Finish this sentence once:

“Most people think X about ___, but they’re wrong because Y.”

Examples (structure only):

- “Most people think consistency means posting daily, but they’re wrong because feedback quality matters more than volume.”
- “Most people think tango is about steps, but they’re wrong because it’s about shared timing.”

Rules

- This is NOT your niche.
- This is your angle.
- You’ll reuse this all month.

Output

- One sentence. Save it.

⏱️ 30–45 min — Define your 3 content pillars

Action

Choose exactly three recurring content types you could talk about for months.

Framework

1. Teach — something you know well
2. Diagnose — common mistakes / myths
3. Demonstrate — your taste, standards, or process

Example (generic)

- Teaching principles
- Breaking down bad advice
- Showing real examples / experiments

Rules

- If a pillar feels vague, it’s wrong.
- If a pillar scares you a little, it’s probably right.

Output

- 3 short labels (5 words max each)

⏱️ 45–55 min — Define “winning” for Week 1

Action

Set process-based, not ego-based goals.

Pick ONE metric

- Posts published (e.g. 5)
- Experiments run (e.g. 3 formats tested)
- Insights captured (e.g. 5 observations)

Write

“Week 1 is successful if I do X.”

No followers. No likes. No cope.
