# Day 7 — Week 1 Integration & Checkpoint

> Week 1 · Foundations · *checkpoint day*

## Learning objective

Run the full Week-1 loop — structure a fresh problem, audit it, prune it — on your own, and get
external feedback to calibrate your self-assessment.

## Minimum Viable Rep — 25 minutes
1. Produce the structure (a full issue tree for a fresh problem you pick).
2. Score it using the rubric.
3. Name one defect.
4. Repair one branch.
5. Write one sentence: "This structure helps because…"

## Concept lesson

Week 1 gave you four moves: choose a cut (Day 3), build an issue tree (Day 4), audit it for
overlaps/gaps/false categories (Day 5), and prune to decision-relevance (Day 6). Today you run
all four on one fresh problem, with no scaffolding telling you which to use when.

The new element is **external feedback**. Self-grading is necessary but it has a blind spot:
you can't see the gap you didn't think of. A second set of eyes — a peer, or an AI you prompt
carefully — catches the overlaps and missing branches you're structurally unable to notice.
This isn't outsourcing the thinking; it's *calibrating* it, so your self-scores become
trustworthy over the next three weeks.

## Practice case

Pick a fresh problem — ideally a real one from `Case-Bank.md` or your own life. Build a complete
issue tree (choose the cut, 2–3 levels), audit it, prune it. Then run the external review and
the failure diary below.

## Deliverable + Definition of Done

A complete, audited, pruned issue tree, plus a completed external review and failure diary.

**Definition of Done:** the tree passes the master checklist; you've incorporated at least one
piece of external feedback; the failure diary is filled honestly; and you can state the decision
the structure improves.

## Self-check rubric

- [ ] I chose the cut deliberately and can say why.
- [ ] The tree is MECE and pruned to decision-relevant branches.
- [ ] **What did external review catch that my self-assessment missed?** (Name it.)
- [ ] I can state the decision or next inquiry this structure improves.
- [ ] My failure diary names a *specific* way I fooled myself, not a generic "I could do better."

## External / AI Review
Paste your deliverable into an AI assistant or show a peer (no private/sensitive details). Ask for:
1. An overlap/gap check.
2. A decision-relevance check.
3. A hidden-assumption check.
4. One stronger alternative structure.
5. What evidence would change the recommendation.
6. Whether the structure is useful or merely tidy.

The goal is calibration, not outsourcing the answer.

## Failure Diary
- This week I fooled myself by…
- The structure looked good because…
- It was actually weak because…
- The repair was…
- Next week I will catch this by…
