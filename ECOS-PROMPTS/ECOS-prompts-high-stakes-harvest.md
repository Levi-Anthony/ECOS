## PROMPT 10 — High-Stakes Conversation Harvest

*Use when: (1) a conversation involves a named person in a complex dynamic,
(2) you're hitting context limits and want maximum extraction before closing,
or (3) you're processing historical conversations during a migration.
Heavier than Prompt 1 — reserve for sessions where the investment justifies it.*

```
Before we close: run a three-pass extraction of everything worth 
preserving in my Open Brain.

PASS 1 — CORE EXTRACTION
Review the full conversation. Identify:
- Decisions made
- Frameworks developed or refined
- Principles clearly articulated
- Strategic directions confirmed
- Anything that would take more than 5 minutes to re-establish 
  in a future session

PASS 2 — LEVI-SPECIFIC SCAN
Now scan specifically for these recurring categories — even if they 
came up incidentally rather than as the main topic:
- Brand voice: new lines, frames, or voice calibrations that landed
- Pedagogy evolution: any shift in how a concept is framed, 
  sequenced, or demonstrated
- Conscious social navigation: anything touching public messaging, 
  subtext, social dynamics, negotiation, rumor or messaging control, 
  inclusion/exclusion dynamics, or personal social power — held with 
  intentionality and in service of something larger
- People context: anything said about a named person worth 
  remembering
- Offer and pricing: decisions or flags with rationale
- Infrastructure and workflow: GHL, Obsidian, Open Brain, capture 
  pipeline — any decision about how the system works
- Losing the plot: any open loop identified, named, or resolved — 
  the "higher self" signal where future-me is being left a mess
- Cross-domain insight: music, teaching, or systems thinking 
  connecting unexpectedly

Pass 2 is additive — only flag items not already caught in Pass 1.

PASS 3 — RELATIONSHIP AND SOCIAL INTELLIGENCE SCAN
Now scan specifically for durable relationship context — patterns and 
intelligence that will be worth having in 6 months when navigating 
interactions with the same people.

For each named person who appears substantively:
- Behavioral patterns: recurring tactics, default responses, 
  predictable escalation triggers
- Relationship dynamics: what kind of relationship this is, 
  what the underlying tension or alignment is
- Strategic navigation: what has worked, what hasn't, what 
  approach is currently calibrated
- Direct observations: specific things they said or did that 
  are worth encoding — not paraphrase, the actual behavior
- Current status: where things stand right now — not history, 
  the present state
- Confidence flag: mark each item HIGH CONFIDENCE (explicitly 
  stated), INFERRED (pattern-based, not stated), or 
  [NEEDS REVIEW] (theoretical, speculative, or model-generated)

Also scan for:
- Social/community dynamics not tied to a single person 
  (group patterns, political dynamics, platform conflicts)
- Strategic lessons from this conversation that generalize 
  beyond the specific relationship

Pass 3 is additive — only flag items not already caught in 
Passes 1 or 2.

OUTPUT FORMAT
Present all proposed entries as a numbered plain text list in chat. 
Do not use tool calls or capture anything yet — I need to review 
and approve each entry first. This is non-negotiable: in this 
interface, tool results are not visible to me.

Write each entry as if leaving a note for a very smart colleague 
who knows me well but wasn't in the room.

Pass 3 items should include the person's name, confidence flag, 
and current status where relevant.

Keep extracting until:
- Each pass has been fully reviewed and no new category yields 
  an additional item
- Remaining candidates fail the standalone test: would this 
  entry be useful retrieved cold, with no other context?
- Remaining candidates fail the generalization test: does this 
  connect to anything active beyond this specific conversation?

Stop before:
- Capturing nuance that only made sense in the moment
- Splitting one solid entry into two thin ones to increase count
- Padding Pass 3 with surface mentions where no behavioral or 
  strategic intelligence was actually present

SKIP
- Tactical to-dos already on a list elsewhere
- Settled, long-standing context unlikely to have shifted
- Anything only relevant to this specific conversation that 
  won't generalize
- Anything superseded or abandoned within the same session
- For Pass 3: surface-level mentions where nothing behavioral 
  or strategic was actually discussed
```
